About

"I didn't take the straight road into software — and I think that's what makes me a better engineer."

Before writing my first React component, I'd managed a 33-acre farm, coordinated logistics for 320+ nonprofit partner agencies, installed solar systems, and built the first AI-assisted analysis tool at a legal tech company. That background taught me something most engineers learn slowly: how to solve real problems with limited resources, communicate across disciplines, and ship things that hold up under pressure.

Today I build full-stack web applications — React and TypeScript on the frontend, Node.js and Express on the backend, PostgreSQL and MongoDB for data. I've shipped production features in a Meteor.js codebase on an agile team, and I bring the same rigor to client work: clean architecture, thoughtful UX, and code that's built to last.

I'm bilingual in English and Spanish, based in Charlotte, NC, and available for engineering roles, freelance builds, and collaborative projects with people who care about what they're making.
